# Paper Seed: Heavy Metal Contamination in Smallholder Farms

## Source
"Soil-water-crop pathways of heavy metal contamination and human health risks
in intensive smallholder farms" Nature Scientific Reports, Iran.

## Domain
Environmental health / soil science / agricultural contamination

## Study design
Cross-sectional field study. 150 smallholder farms across 3 cropping systems
(irrigated wheat, rice paddies, orchards). Soil, irrigation water, and crop
samples analyzed for heavy metals. Health risk assessment calculated.

## Variables
- Heavy metal concentrations in soil: Cd, Pb, Zn, Cu, Cr, Ni, As
- Heavy metal concentrations in irrigation water
- Heavy metal concentrations in crops (grain, fruit, vegetable)
- Soil pH, organic matter, texture
- Irrigation source (well, canal, river)
- Cropping system (wheat, rice, orchard)
- Transfer factor (soil → crop)
- Hazard quotient (health risk per metal)
- Hazard index (cumulative health risk)
- Distance to industrial sources
- Fertilizer use intensity
- Years of cultivation

## Key findings
- Rice paddies had highest Cd accumulation (anaerobic conditions increase mobility)
- Irrigation water from canal had highest contamination
- Health risk exceeded safe levels for Cd and As in some farms
- Soil organic matter moderated metal bioavailability
- Distance to industrial source was strongest predictor of soil contamination

## Research questions
- Which pathway (soil, water, crop) contributes most to human exposure?
- Does cropping system affect metal accumulation differently for each metal?
- Is the health risk above acceptable thresholds?
- What's the dominant source: irrigation water, fertilizers, or industrial proximity?
- Can soil properties (pH, organic matter) mitigate contamination risk?

## Causal complexity
- Multiple pathways: industrial → soil → crop → human, industrial → water → crop → human
- Confounders: farms near industry also tend to have more intensive agriculture
- Mediators: soil pH mediates metal bioavailability
- Interaction: cropping system × metal type × soil property
- Heterogeneity: different metals behave differently (Cd mobile, Pb immobile)
- Spatial: distance decay from source, but non-uniform due to topography/wind
